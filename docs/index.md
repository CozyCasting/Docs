# Welcome to CozyCasting

CozyCasting is a relaxing fishing game for Discord. Cast your line, catch unique fish, build your collection, explore fishing spots around the world, and join guilds with other players — all from the comfort of your server.

## Features

- **Catch fish** across 9 location types and 3 geographic regions
- **Deploy nets** to passively catch fish over time while you are away
- **Collect rarities** from Common to Mythical, each with unique traits
- **Upgrade your gear** with rods and bait that improve your odds
- **Level up** and unlock new locations, achievements, and titles
- **Build your camp** for passive income
- **Found or join guilds** with other players to unlock shared bonuses and build guild camps
- **Open chests** for loot drops including bait, consumables, and titles
- **Compete** on server leaderboards
